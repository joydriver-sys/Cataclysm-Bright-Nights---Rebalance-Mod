# Cataclysm-Bright-Nights---Rebalance-Mod
A mod for Cataclysm: Bright Nights: a grittier, more challenging, and more lethal Cataclysm.

# Lore

"The government has fallen. You are on your own."

The world you used to live in was a fantastical one. Atomic-powered cars, consumer-available cyborg modifications, and high-tech fabrication and 3d printing were right at your fingertips. If those things somehow didn't meet your wants and needs, the independent mage schools and formal magical orders were there to fill in the gaps. 

And yet: all of them have now fallen. The super soldier brigades. The power armored legions. Even the spell-slinging arch-mages. 

As your supplies dwindle, you realize that you'll need to scavenge the remnants of all the disparate wonders your former world had to offer, from the high-tech to the arcane. You clutch your cudgel a little more tightly as you approach the nearest city, and the sound of screams and roars reach your ears. 

# Description

This mod is a patch for the base Bright Nights game, along with the Arcana, C.R.I.T., Magical Nights, Cata++ and Project: Packrat content mods. It is not intended for the base game alone, and is balanced with the assumption that at least one or two and more likely multiple content mods are enabled for the playthrough. 

The overall effect of the mod is to make cities and labs much more deadly and dangerous by increasing the damage output of zombies and turrets, while simultaneously reducing the player's ability to become impervious to damage by stacking armor, and reducing stat and speed bonuses from many sources. You can still become a bulletproof juggernaut of destruction that walks into automatic 5.56 gunfire without fear, but that goal is significantly more difficult to achieve. 

The gameplay intent is for players to be incentivized to engage with Bright Nights as an immersive sim rather than a straightforward zombie slaughter. Holding tab with a decent weapon and armor no longer does the job (at least not for a while), so you'll need to use guns, spells, vehicles, bombs, and other methods to survive. 

# Changelog

- The armor value of all materials has been cut in half (rounded up). 
- Hardcoded bullet resistance on most items has also been cut in half. (The bullet resistance of power armor is unchanged.)
- The armor value of bionic alloy plating is reduced.
- Stat and speed bonuses from bionics, gear, and spells has been reduced.
- Zombie and feral melee skill has been increased by approximately 75%. 
- Zombies and ferals roll additional damage dice, and ignore 40% of armor. 
- Zombies use special attacks more often, and zombie bites have light armor penetration.
- Zombies and ferals have increased grab strength, scaling with type. (Basic -> Tough -> Soldier/Blackops -> Hulks and Grabbers)
- Zombie and feral base HP increased by approximately 25%.
- Light, Medium, and Long Range milspec turrets have had their ammunition calibers increased by one step each. 9mm->5.56, 5.56->7.62, 7.62->.50 Cal

# Not-changed log

- It should be noted that mutations are completely untouched, along with bionics such as Subdermal Carbon Filament and Active Defense System. This makes post-threshold armor mutations very attractive options for their encumbrance values. 

# Testing

To test the gameplay changes, I rolled two characters and played with the rebalance mods enabled. Each of them had 8 in all stats, and the following traits: Indefatiguable, Pain Resistant, Scout, Packrat, Strong Back. The first character was a basic Survivor, and died to a small mob of zombies in late Spring. The second character was a HazMat, and has survived to early fall so far. Based on these runs, I judged the changes to be quite challenging, but very playable. 

# Recommended Settings and Content Mods

I recommend playing with:

Cata++
C.R.I.T. Expansion
Exotic Ammo Types
Aftershock
Project: Packrat
RPG System
Arcana and Magic Items
Magical Nights

Along with the main Rebalance mod and all of its patch mods. 

I recommend starting with no more than 8 points per stat (distributed however you like), and RPG System level progression set to 50%. 

For basic traits, I recommend Indefatiguable, Pain Resistant, and Packrat at minimum. Thick Skin and Fast Healer will make the early game much, much less punishing. I don't recommend taking the basic mana traits: RPG System allows you to scale up to more than 7000 mana with crazy amounts of regen, so it's just not necessary. 

For RPG System traits, Tireless and Packrat are both solid picks. Tireless gives you a lot more stamina that you can use to sprint away from bad engagements, and Packrat allows you to use a much lighter backpack or forgo one altogether, making room for more armor.
